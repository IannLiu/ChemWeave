04 Single-event annotation Optional

Calculate n_e directly from each complete mapped reaction. Works with your own templates and species; no family whitelist or extra recipe.

Catalytic example: H₂ + 2 M → 2 MH. Loads hydrogen and explicit metal sites; click Generate to calculate n_e = 2 / 2! = 1.

The factor is the number of distinct labelled events divided once by the identical-reactant factorial. Surface * nodes default to abstract X; optional attributes distinguish site types.

Calculation options and imported factors (JSON)

Options: calculator, factors, parameters. Imported factors select reaction_id and a 1-based channel, with factor, convention and source. Parameter classes and kinetic units are optional legacy metadata.
